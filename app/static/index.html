    How to read Δ and p
      
    Δ is absolute degradation in percentage points: reference − ablated,
        so positive means the ablation hurt. A non-positive Δ on intent-only means the
        classes separate without the injection — a corpus shortcut, not detection.
        ns marks a Δ whose paired McNemar test (Holm-corrected, same frozen test rows)
        is not significant; hover any Δ for its exact p.

    Instructions
    
      
      
        
    • Needs a run with saved classifiers. Run tab → keep save models checked → they land in
          runs/<pipeline>/<mode>/models/*.joblib. Without them this tab stays disabled.
      • 
        
    • Both fields matter in normal mode: the context and the intent are joined with a separator
          and embedded as one text, exactly as the pipeline did at training time.
      • 
        
    • minilm embeds locally in milliseconds. openai needs OPENAI_API_KEY in
          .env. qwen3 loads a 4B model into VRAM on first use — slow first call, fast after.
      • 
        
    • random row pulls a labeled row from the dataset; its ground truth stays hidden until you detect,
          so you can guess first.
      • 
        
    • Verdict is the classifier's probability at a 0.50 threshold; the exact confidence prints under the button.
